Are the fragrance notes final?+

The full compositions supplied for Khwab, Sukoon and Iraada are now shown in every product detail view, from top through heart to base.

Each full bottle is 50 ml at ₹2,499. The discovery box contains all three fragrances in 8 ml bottles and is shown at ₹1,299.

Can I buy from this prototype?+

You can explore, take the scent finder and test the bag experience. Payment is not active; Shopify checkout will replace the prototype checkout at launch.

Yes. The First Three discovery box contains 3 × 8 ml bottles: Khwab, Sukoon and Iraada.

Yes. The prototype is deliberately section-based, and the product, cart, review and email interactions have direct Shopify equivalents.
